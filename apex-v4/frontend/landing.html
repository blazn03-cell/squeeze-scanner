Under the hood

The layers that produce a score.

Nine layers run on every symbol, every scan. Three more are built into the data model but stay dark until a licensed options feed is connected — they're marked as such rather than filled with guesses.

Running on every scan

Live

Technical structure

EMA 9/20/50 stack, VWAP position, ADX and directional index, RSI — the skeleton of the trend.

Market regime

Each name is classified: strong trend, squeeze building, breakout, high volatility, reversal or chop. Context before signal.

Squeeze context

Bollinger/Keltner compression with state, bar count and fire detection — plus a historical hit-rate replay of the same objective condition.

Relative volume

Volume against its own 30-day baseline, banded — so a heavy tape on a quiet name isn't hidden by a big name's raw volume.

Volatility sizing

ATR% banded from dead to extreme. Names too quiet to move and too wild to size are both penalized.

Liquidity

Live bid/ask spread as a percentage. Wide spreads cut the score directly; missing quotes are marked CHECK, never assumed tight.

Event risk

Days to earnings. Inside the danger window the score is multiplied down by up to 40% — a good chart in front of a print is not a good setup.

Risk framework

Entry zone, volatility or structure stop, 2R target, and risk as a percentage of entry — computed per row, not drawn by hand.

Setup quality ranking

Everything above is weighted into one 0–100 score, then sorted, so the top of the list is the top of the list for a stated reason.

Modeled, waiting on the licensed options feed

Not live

Dark pool activity

Off-exchange print volume and level clustering. Requires a licensed feed — shown as locked rather than approximated from candles.

Institutional options flow

Ask-side sweeps, block prints and strike concentration. The current Smart Flow column is a price/volume proxy and is labeled as one.

Gamma exposure

Dealer positioning and gamma flip levels. Candles alone can never establish gamma positioning, so the engine refuses to claim it.

Straight answers

What this is not.

⚠ Beta — research and paper trading only

Profitability has not been demonstrated. The historical evidence sets collected so far have confidence intervals that include zero, and the ticker selection in those replays carries survivorship bias. Before any non-beta claim is made, the project requires:

  • At least 100 forward paper observations per frozen configuration
  • After-cost confidence intervals above zero, clustered by symbol
  • No single symbol contributing more than 20% of positive aggregate R
  • Acceptable slippage across multiple market regimes

A score is a technical ranking of setup quality — not a probability of profit, a win rate, or a recommendation. The engine ranks the underlying stock; it does not select or validate an option contract, and it has no live option bid/ask, delta, IV, or open-interest validation. Entry, stop and target are reference levels derived from ATR and recent structure, not instructions. Squeeze and flush labels describe price and volume conditions — they are not proof of short covering, and a gamma label is never inferred from candles. Dark pool, options flow and gamma columns stay locked until a licensed feed supplies them. No part of this system places orders or connects to a brokerage. Educational use only.
